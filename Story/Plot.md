# Outline
To escape war, a northman suffering from PTSD is pressured by his brother to sail with him for a new land. After arriving, he must overcome his fears as they attempt to establish a farmstead without succumbing to the horrors that dwell there.

## 1. Forest Exploration
The chapter starts out with Ljósbjǫrn, Alfarr (his brother), and a few other people working on a new longhouse. He complains that he is exhausted and wants to rest, especially after the two-week trip across the ocean. When he goes to sit down, he and his brother are approached by a messenger, who tells them that Hallgrímr has requested a forest exploration. He is reluctant, but goes anyway.

When the two arrive at the community hall, they meet up with Sága and Ingólfr. They wait for one more, who is named Rúna. After that, the five of them set out into the forest. As they walk, Sága asks Alfarr whether Ljósbjǫrn is cursed. Alfarr gets offended by this and retaliates. After some silence, Rúna asks him vaguely if he "really is", and he gets confused, but quickly realises and changes the subject by asking for her name.

They enter the forest and discover crowberries in a glade. While doing so, Ljósbjǫrn cannot help but notice movement in the forest, but soon realizes it's just a deer. This riles him up, and he becomes paranoid, leading him to discover an overgrown path.

Soon, they approach an oddly-shaped hill surrounded by mist, which is actually a mound. Rúna doesn't approach it. Ljósbjǫrn hesitates, but he goes in since he doesn't want to be called cowardly. Alfarr finds a goblet and picks it up, thinking he has found treasure. But Ljósbjǫrn sees what his brother has done and immediately becomes suspicious and angry, saying that Alfarr shouldn't have done that.

## 2. The Seeress
After nightfall, Ljósbjǫrn is finishing his meal, enjoying a warm stew after the bitterness of dried, salted fish. Alfarr approaches him and says that the local seeress has been eyeing him since they came back, and that he heard a commotion in the community hall between Hallgrímr and the seeress. They both agree to go eavesdrop.

While the two stand outside the windows, they hear the seeress saying something about woe. They realise she is talking about the goblet when she mentions it. Alfarr then tries to go in and get the goblet back, but the seeress stops him, saying that he doesn't understand and has woken something. Hallgrímr, however, isn't having it and decides to excuse Alfarr.

## 3. Unlikely Disputes
Ljósbjǫrn feels sick from the new environment and decides he probably needs a walk, so he puts on his armour and walks around the perimeter. While walking near the forest, he discovers a stake in the ground. He immediately suspects something is wrong and stares at it.

Once again, he sees movement in the forest and quickly hides as he realises it's a person. But it's just Rúna foraging for some mushrooms. He goes up to her and sternly tells her she should not be in the forest. He then asks what she's doing, to which she reveals. Seeing nothing wrong with it, he decides to disregard it and continue his walk.

When he returns, he sees a dispute between several trolls and some of the Vikings' armed men. Apparently, they were caught sneaking in the bushes. Sága, however, thought this was a wonderful moment, so she tried to trade with the trolls. They offer a necklace of obsidian beads. But when she offers them a knife, they get offended since it's made of iron and leave.

## 4. Plague of Illness
People start falling into sickness. First, it was Hallgrímr. Then, it was Sága, Æskell, and Alfarr. Weeks pass, and Ljósbjǫrn decides to go visit Alfarr, who is being treated inside the community hall. Ragnhildr the Halfgiant, a gruff-looking woman, is Alfarr's healer. She denies Ljósbjǫrn, who wants to go inside. However, he finds his brother by speaking to him through a window.

While they talk, Ljósbjǫrn hears a commotion inside. Ingólfr is trying to take over for Hallgrímr, who is refusing his substitution. Ingólfr leaves frustrated and angry, prompting Ljósbjǫrn to leave too so that he doesn't get caught.

## 5. An Unseen Visit
Ljósbjǫrn begins to feel the sickness grow on him. He seeks Rúna out for answers since he already has a bad impression of Ragnhildr. She says she doesn't know what to do and just suggests walking on the beach, but then he hears the settlers' war horn and goes to investigate.

He meets with Alfarr, who is equally confused. They begin to see warriors gather in the direction of the forest. Ljósbjǫrn asks a nearby bowman what is going on, and the bowman reveals that one of their lookouts has spotted a troll.

Ljósbjǫrn goes there and watches the lookout man shoot two arrows blindly into the forest. Ingólfr, who is there, attempts to rally the warriors into doing a search, but they all show disinterest.







## 7. Troll Attack
A band of trolls attack the settlement in an attempt to take control of it. They specifically target Hallgrímr and attempt to kill him. But they cannot. Ljósbjǫrn fights the trolls, but he is cornered by two of them inside a house. A troll shaman joins the fight and chants something, causing a beam to break and strike Ljósbjǫrn. He falls unconscious.

## 8. Captive
Ljósbjǫrn wakes up, gagged and bound. He is lying inside what appears to be a tent made of animal pelts, without his weapons. As he panics, he hears a human's voice mixed with several haunting voices. They approach the tent, and a troll enters.

The troll takes his gag off and interrogates him, saying that he is "haunted" and that he should know how the goblet works. Ljósbjǫrn says he does not know what he is talking about. However, the troll is persistent. After multiple failures, the troll threatens him with starvation if he doesn't comply and leaves.



## 12. Draugr's Revelation
During the night, Ljósbjǫrn wakes up to the subtle sound of something strange moving outside. Grabbing his spear and a hand candle, he creeps outside despite knowing that he shouldn't. He finds a runaway goat. But he notices something strange about it. The goat is awake, isn't bleating, and is walking exclusively on the paved road.

Thinking it's one of Thor's goats, he decides to go back in. However, he accidentally kicks a bucket and quickly gets onto his stomach, hiding. The goat comes closer.
