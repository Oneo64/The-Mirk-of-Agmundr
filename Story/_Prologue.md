# Prologue
The year was 922 when two longboats had their full sails flapping, carried north by the ocean gale. The sun was low. Not a single bird flew in the sky, not even the sound of their call. On one of the ships, they had animals and barrels of food. And amongst the crew, a blond-haired man sat on his box.

The blond man was staring at his amulet. It was Thor's hammer. His fingers trembled slightly as he held the cold iron symbol, hoping it would protect him. He stared at it for a moment, then tucked it back into his cloak.

The flapping sail bore a red colour with a blue cross, and a rough texture from the thick flax fibres. A wolf’s skull hung from the bow’s tip, swaying and knocking on the wood.

The blond man looked up into the sky. There was no use smelling the air for an oceanic scent; it was everywhere. But by the murk of the clouds, it was going to rain. He turned his gaze towards the thing they were sailing towards. It was a stripe of a dark colour, but he knew what it was: land.

"Ljǫrn," said a low voice behind him. "Are you still thinking about Álahringr?"

The blond man, who was named Ljǫrn, looked around to see his brother leaning towards him. Despite his voice mostly drained by the wind, he could tell his brother was amused. His brother's face reminded him of the time when his brother persuaded him to join Hallgrímr's company. He had initially refused to go, but the thought of peace and wealth compelled him, and so here he was.

Ljǫrn didn't respond for a moment. "Alfarr," he sighed, his voice low. "I—"

"I told you this would make us rich," said his brother, unintentionally cutting off the words of the blond man. "They're going to Fjǫrðvík, father is. You don't need to worry."

Ljǫrn placed his gaze back onto the land. "We are near," he mumbled.

"The wind falters!" a voice remarked loudly, clear even in the wind. Ljǫrn and Alfarr both looked at the person standing in front. It was Hallgrímr. His black hair and beard made him ferocious. "Sails up! Oars ready! Prepare to row!"
