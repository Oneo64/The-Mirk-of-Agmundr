# Fjǫrðvík
Fjǫrðvík is a village built atop a tall headland in southeastern Iceland, the cliff acting as a natural barrier against Viking raids. The village gets its name from the scenic view of many jagged headlands along the coast.

## Etymology
The name Fjǫrðvík comes from [fjǫrðr](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/fjǫrðr#Old_Norse) *("fjord")* + [vík](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/vík#Old_Norse) *("bay")*.
