# Hrafnhamarr
Hrafnhamarr is a coastal village in eastern Newfoundland. It was established some time around AD 900 as a small farmstead. Surrounding the settlement is a pine forest, with a river flowing downhill from the forest to the ocean.

## Etymology
The name Hrafnhamarr comes from [hrafn](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/hrafn#Old_Norse) *("raven")* + [hamarr](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/hamarr#Old_Norse) *("crag")*.
