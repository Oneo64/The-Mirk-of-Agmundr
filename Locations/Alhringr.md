# Álhringr
Álhringr is a coastal village and a major trade location in southwestern Iceland. Tens of longhouses are built in the village, connected by roads which eventually lead away to other distant villages.

A birch and ash forest borders the village. Deeper in the forest, the landscape becomes steeper and rockier, and the trees grow taller and darker. Somewhere inside the forest, there is a river in a dip in the ground, which eventually flows to the ocean.

## Etymology
The name Álhringr comes from [áll](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/áll#Old_Norse) *("a dip in the Earth from a dried river")* + [hringr](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/hringr#Old_Norse) *("ring, community")*.
