Hallbjǫrn the Uncoverer was a Viking explorer who led a group of settlers to Agmundarland, where they established a settlement. He is now a chieftain, overseeing his place with pride.

## Appearance
Hallbjǫrn has a lean body and is around 5'9" in height. His hair is black with grey streaks, paired with a full, scruffy beard. His green eyes are deep-set and have a naturally withered shape, giving him a scary appearance.

Hallbjǫrn wears a dark cloak, a muted red tunic, dark pants, a ring belt, and a pair of shoes. In combat, he wears a maille hauberk and spectacle helmet with a maille mask.

## Personality
Hallbjǫrn is arrogant, rowdy, and talkative. He is stubborn and argumentative, usually defending anything he believes until he is directly proven wrong.

As an explorer, Hallbjǫrn is very adventurous, wanting to see something for himself rather than merely listening to stories of it. Because of his explorative personality, he is willing to join any adventuring or exploring party just to experience the "old stories".

However, Hallbjǫrn also recognises his duty as a leader and must restrain himself from going on expedition parties.

## Abilities
Hallbjǫrn is intimidating as a warrior, fighting his enemies with a Dane axe. He usually takes a defensive stance when in combat, taunting his foes to strike if they cower.
