Ingólfr was the leader of the first exploration team that set out after they arrived in Agmundarland.

## Appearance
Ingólfr has long dark brown hair, upturned hazel eyes, and pale skin. He is tall and lean.

Ingólfr wears a brown cloak, a muted red and green tunic, brown pants, a buckled belt, and a pair of shoes. He wears a Mjǫllnir necklace and has a sword and scabbard. When travelling, he dons a hauberk and a round shield.

## Personality
Ingólfr is a brave warrior striving to become a true leader. He is fearless, often being the first to charge with a war cry. However, he can become manipulative and try to steer situations to his benefit, such as controlling conversations and plans by speaking calmly and confidently.
