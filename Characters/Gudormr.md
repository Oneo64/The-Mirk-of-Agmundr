# Guðormr
Guðormr was a Norse Viking who raided an English village and brought a sword back home. He is the father of Aðalgeirr and the grandfather of Úlfnir, Ljósbjǫrn, and Alfarr.

## Trivia
- Guðormr's name comes from [guð](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/guð#Old_Norse) *("god")* + [ormr](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/ormr#Old_Norse) *("serpent")*.
