# Karl Ljósbjarnarson
Karl is the son of Ljósbjǫrn and Rúna, who appears a decade and a half after the main events of the Mirk of Agmundr. He is 11 years old and is depicted with light brown, wavy hair, downturned blue eyes, and freckles across his cheeks.

## Trivia
- Karl's name means "man" in Old Norse.
