# Mjaðveig
Mjaðveig is the mother of Ljósbjǫrn, Alfarr, and the late eldest brother, Úlfnir. She has long brown hair and brown eyes, and wears a red woollen dress.

## Trivia
- Mjaðveig's name comes from [mjǫðr](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/mjǫðr#Old_Norse) *("mead")* + [-veig](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/veig#Old_Norse) *("strength")*.
