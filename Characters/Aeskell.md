# Æskell
Æskell is one of Hallgrímr's housecarls who fell sick to the draugr's powers.

## Trivia
- The name Æskell is a shortened variant of Ásketill, which comes from [áss](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/áss#Old_Norse) *("god")* + [ketill](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/ketill#Old_Norse) *("cauldron, helmet")*.
