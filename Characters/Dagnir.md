# Dagnir
Dagnir is a settler and a "scoundrel" in Hallgrímr's company. He has long black hair, a scruffy beard, and brown eyes, and he wears a brown cloak with a dark yellow tunic. People in Hrafnhamarr call him shady since he's always lurking around after dark. The only equipment he owns is a hatchet and a seax.
