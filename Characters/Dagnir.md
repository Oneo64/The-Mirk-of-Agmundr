# Dagnir
Dagnir is a settler in Hallgrímr's company who has long black hair, a scruffy beard, and brown eyes. He wears a ragged cloak and dark clothing, which helps him sneak around in the dark. The only weapons he owns are a hatchet and a seax.

People in Hrafnhamarr call Dagnir shady since he's always lurking around after dark, with some even going as far as to call him a scoundrel. He is a loner since he doesn't seem to have any relatives or friends because of his reputation.
