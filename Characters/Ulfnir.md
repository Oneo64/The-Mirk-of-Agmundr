# Úlfnir Aðalgeirsson
Úlfnir was the brother of Ljósbjǫrn and Alfarr. Because he was the firstborn son of the family, his father passed his sword down to him. He was slain by a Viking who struck him in the neck with an axe.

## Trivia
- Úlfnir's name comes from [úlfr](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/úlfr#Old_Norse) *("wolf")* + -nir.
