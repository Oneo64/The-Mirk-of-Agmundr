# Kolbrandr
Kolbrandr was the local blacksmith of Álahringr. He didn't have a particularly strong relationship with anyone except Ljósbjǫrn, a local boy who would sneak into his workplace whenever he came off. He had always suspected someone was going through his things, but he could never catch them.

Unfortunately, on one fateful night, a Viking raid happened in the village, and Kolbrandr's house burned down. He was never found again.

## Trivia
- Kolbrandr's name comes from [kolr](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/kolr#Old_Norse) *("coal")* + [brandr](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/brandr#Old_Norse) *("fire, sword")*.
