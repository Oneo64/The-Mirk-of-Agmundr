# Draugr
A draugr is a powerful undead monster possessing unique abilities. They are usually people who died and weren't buried properly, or who had strong motivations before death. Since they don't fully rot after death, they retain their intelligence and skills. They are usually described as emitting a foul smell and having the appearance of a half-decayed corpse.

One can assess a tomb's conditions to determine whether it has become the home of a draugr. For example, a decomposing smell or the stench of death combined with dry air means that the corpse hasn't rotted away yet. Strange dreams whilst sleeping near the tomb or belongings being moved overnight mean that the corpse may be active.

To prevent someone from becoming a draugr after they die, the body should be properly laid down with all their most valuable belongings. If someone were to take extreme measures, the body would be moved out of a temporary door built into the house where they died, moved head-first into their tomb, and then their legs would be bound together.

There are many ways a draugr can be destroyed, but the most common methods are burning the corpse, destroying their head, or decapitating them and placing the head between their groin.
